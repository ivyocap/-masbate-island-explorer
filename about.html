01 — THE PLACE

An island province
with its own rhythm.

Masbate is an island province in the Bicol Region of the Philippines, known for its coastal landscapes, island communities, and distinctive local culture.

Its identity is shaped by the sea, the land, and the people who call it home. Whether you are interested in nature, food, festivals, or simply seeing somewhere new, Masbate offers plenty to appreciate.
